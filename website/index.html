Manual install (developer mode)

  1. Download and unzip KAVACH-extension.zip.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Does KAVACH send my browsing history anywhere?

No. All scoring happens locally in your browser. Your cache and allowlist are stored only on your device using browser storage.

What if a safe website gets blocked?

Click Access the Site on the warning page to continue, and tick Permanently trust this domain to add it to your allowlist. You can also use Trust This Site from the toolbar popup.

Which browsers are supported?

Any Chromium-based browser that supports Manifest V3: Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Opera and Vivaldi.

Can I pause protection?

Yes. Use the switch in the popup to pause or resume protection at any time, and change the cache duration from 5 to 60 minutes.

Why does it need access to all websites?

To check each page address before it loads and to show the warning page, KAVACH needs permission to see the URLs you navigate to. It does not read page content or form data.
