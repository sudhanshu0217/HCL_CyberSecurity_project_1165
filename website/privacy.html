KAVACH Privacy Policy

KAVACH analyses the address (URL) of each page you open, locally in your browser, to calculate a phishing risk score.

  • No browsing data, URLs or personal information are collected, sold or sent to any server.
  • Settings, the temporary scan cache and your trusted-site list are stored only on your device using browser storage.
  • KAVACH does not read page content, form data or passwords.

The "all websites" permission is used only to check each URL before it loads and to show the warning page.
